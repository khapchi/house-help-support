House Help Tracker – Support

Thank you for using House Help Tracker, a free mobile app to manage your maids, house-helps, and daily subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my data stored?
    All data is stored securely on your device. No cloud sync or external server is involved.
  • Can I back up my data?
    Backup and export features are under development. For now, your data is tied to your phone storage.
  • How do I disable or delete a service?
    Use the Manage Services screen in the app to disable or permanently delete any service.
