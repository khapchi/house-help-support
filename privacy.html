Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2025

House Help Tracker values your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your data:

1. Data Storage

This app stores all service and attendance data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your data externally.

2. No Analytics or Advertising

We do not use third-party analytics tools or display ads. Your usage data is never tracked or sold.

3. Contacting Support

If you email us for support, your email address and message are used solely for responding to your request.

4. Data Deletion

You may delete all app data at any time by clearing app storage from your device settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. We recommend checking this page for the latest version.
